How it works

Three things, kept deliberately simple

01 — Connect

Meet your cohort

Everyone from your university heading to the same city, same term, gets grouped together — before anyone lands. Find a roommate, split a taxi from the airport, stop guessing who else is going.

02 — Learn

Guides that aren't generic

SIM cards, bank accounts, housing near campus, what things actually cost — written by students who just did your exact route, not a travel blog written for anyone and no one.

03 — Save

Deals worth having

As each route's group grows, local partners — housing, insurance, SIM — compete for a group of verified incoming students instead of guessing at random travelers.

Where this actually stands: this just launched. There's no giant network yet — it's being built by a student in Madrid who's watched friends scatter to Barcelona, Florence, and beyond every semester with zero way to find each other first. Sign up and you help kick off your own route's community.
